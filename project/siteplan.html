Exploring Japan: Culture, Landmarks, and Travel Guide

Site Name

Exploring Japan

This name was chosen because it clearly represents the purpose of the site: discovering Japan’s culture, famous landmarks, and travel opportunities in an engaging way.

Optional domain: exploringjapan.com

Site Purpose

The website will provide an informative and visually engaging guide to Japan. It will introduce Japanese culture, highlight popular landmarks, and offer useful travel tips. It will also include a weather feature and a contact form for user interaction.

Scenarios

  • What are the must-visit landmarks when traveling to Japan?
  • What cultural practices should I know before visiting Japan?
  • What is the weather like in Japan right now?

Color Schema

  • Red (#c8102e): Used for header, headings, and accents.
  • Light Gray (#f5f5f5): Used for background.
  • Dark Gray (#333333): Used for body text.

Typography

  • Playfair Display: Used for headings.
  • Noto Sans: Used for body text.
